HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to a stronger start.

1

Pick your missions

Set your wake-up time and choose what you’ll do: squats, push-ups, plank, making your bed, opening the curtains, a glass of water or your own mission. You set the count.

2

Move to stop the alarm

Put your phone down and go. The camera recognises your movements and counts along, with spoken encouragement. Done? The alarm stops.

3

Build your streak

Every morning counts. Watch your streak grow, earn badges and track your weekly goal. Miss a day and your monthly joker has your back.

LEVELS

You choose how strict Mooove is.

Free

A regular alarm with stop button and snooze. Nice for an easy start.

Flexible

Move your alarm by 15, 30 or 60 minutes when you need a bit more time.

Snoozable

Snooze up to three times. Then it’s mission time.

Mandatory

No snooze. The alarm only stops when your mission is done.

Hardcore

Mission required, plus a wake-up check after five minutes. Back to bed is not an option.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does the alarm ring when my phone is on silent?

Yes. Mooove uses your phone’s alarm system (AlarmKit on iPhone, exact alarms on Android). The Alarm check in the app makes sure everything is set up.

What if I can’t move, or I’m injured?

Pick a mission that suits you, like a glass of water or opening the curtains, or set the level to Free. At every level, the emergency stop always stops the alarm. Mooove is not a medical device: move within your own limits.

Are my camera images stored?

No. Recognition happens on your own phone. Images are never stored or sent. Even the photo confirming your made bed is deleted straight away.

Which phones does Mooove work on?

iPhones with iOS 26 or later and Android phones with Android 8 or later.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, in the app via Profile → Delete account. All your data is deleted immediately and permanently. You can download all your data first, too.
